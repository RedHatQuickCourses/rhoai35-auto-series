From Raw Data to Ranked Models

Estimated reading time: 10 minutes.

Objective

Recognize the four AutoML prediction task types and the business economics that make automated model generation compelling.

Not started yet.

The business case for AutoML

At the C-Suite level, AutoML compresses model development from months to hours, delivering 70–80% faster time-to-market and acting as a 3x workforce multiplier. Think of AutoML as a "Sorting Hat" that converts raw CSVs pulled from S3 into a ranked leaderboard of candidate models.

Choosing a prediction task type

Every predictive problem maps to one of four task types:

  • Binary — a "Yes/No" decision.

  • Multiclass — choosing from a "menu" of categories.

  • Regression — answering "How much?".

  • Time Series — predicting the "next value over time".

Sizing economics

Predictive models are cheap to run: microsecond CPU inference with KB/MB-sized artifacts, in sharp contrast to GPU-hungry LLMs. Understanding this cost profile is key to justifying where AutoML fits in an enterprise portfolio.
