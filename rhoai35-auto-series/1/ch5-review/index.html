Knowledge Review and Assessment

Goal

Track core enablement concepts across AutoML, MLflow prompt tracking, and AutoRAG to validate practitioner capability in diagnosing enterprise data scenarios and selecting optimal AI patterns.

Four capabilities, one platform. Here’s the thing though: in a real enterprise, you rarely use just one of them. The interesting work happens when they combine, when a predictive model and a grounded generator sit in the same workflow and hand data back and forth. That’s what this final chapter is about. First we’ll walk one realistic end-to-end scenario so you can see the pieces click together, then we’ll sharpen the decision logic you’ll actually be graded on: which capability fits which problem, how to read the metrics when something looks off, and, just as important, when "Auto" is enough and when it isn’t.

After that comes the graded assessment. It’s scenario-based on purpose. Nobody’s going to quiz you on trivia; the questions put you in the seat of someone diagnosing a data problem and asking "which pattern do I reach for, and how do I know it’s working?"

We’ll do it in two moves: a synthesis section that connects everything into a single workflow and hands you a one-page decision cheat sheet, then the quiz itself.
