Connecting Data to Models: The End-to-End Workflow

Estimated reading time: 12 minutes.

Objective

Combine AutoML, MLflow, and AutoRAG into a single decision workflow and reason about when automation alone is sufficient.

Not started yet.

End-to-end workflow synergy

The capabilities in this course combine into a live business workflow: AutoML risk scores plus AutoRAG-generated credit memos power a loan officer’s decision loop, showing predictive and generative AI working together.

Diagnostic decision logic

Metric combinations tell a diagnostic story. For example, High Faithfulness combined with Low Correctness signals that the retrieval pipeline is missing document context rather than hallucinating.

When "Auto" is enough

Establish clear decision gates for staying with automated capabilities versus escalating to SLM fine-tuning (the subject of Course 2). Knowing where "Auto" stops is as important as knowing what it can do.

What’s Next

With the full workflow and decision logic in view, the final section is a scenario-based graded assessment that validates your ability to diagnose enterprise data scenarios and select the optimal AI pattern.
