Welcome to chapter four, Generative Retrieval with Auto RAG.

So what is Auto RAG? It's an automated optimization engine. It benchmarks your chunking, embedding, and vector database configurations for grounded large language model applications, and hands you back the combination that works best.

The easiest way to picture it: Auto RAG applies the idea of Auto M L specifically to retrieval. Instead of tuning by hand, you provide your documents and a set of test questions. Auto RAG searches across different chunking, embedding, and retrieval configurations, scores each one against your questions, and hands back a ranked leaderboard of production-ready RAG patterns.

One thing to know up front: Auto RAG is currently a Technology Preview feature in OpenShift AI 3.5.

We've included a demonstration that walks through the whole process using a scenario from a fictitious bank. I'd watch it once now to get a feel for the overall workflow; we'll unpack each stage in the lessons that follow.

We'll cover this in three moves. One, how to prepare the two essential inputs Auto RAG needs. Two, the search space it explores, and how to read the metrics it reports back. And three, how to inspect the winning configuration and export it into a real application.
