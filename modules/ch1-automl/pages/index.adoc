= Predictive Foundations with AutoML

Goal::

Evaluate when to use AutoML to convert raw tabular data or time series into production-ready predictive models across classification, regression, and time-series tasks.

---

AutoML in Red Hat OpenShift AI 3.5+ takes the raw tabular data sitting in your S3 bucket, automates the tedious middle (data prep, feature engineering, algorithm selection, and ensembling), and hands you back a ranked leaderboard of production-ready candidate models. Not in months, but in hours.

This chapter is about the *foundations*: why this matters to the business, where predictive AI fits alongside the LLMs everyone's talking about, how to tell whether your data is even a good fit. How to match any business problem to the right kind of prediction. By the end you won't be running models yet, that comes later, but you'll know exactly when AutoML is the right tool to reach for, and why.
