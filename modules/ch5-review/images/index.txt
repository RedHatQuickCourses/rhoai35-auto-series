Welcome to this final knowledge review and assessment.

The primary goal of this session is to track your understanding of core enablement concepts across AutoML, prompt management, and AutoRAG. This will validate your capability to diagnose enterprise data scenarios and select the most effective AI patterns.

The most compelling work in this field happens when these technologies converge—specifically, when a predictive model and a grounded generator operate within the same workflow, passing data back and forth. That is the focus of this final chapter.

We will proceed in two moves. First, we will walk through a realistic, end-to-end scenario so you can see how all these pieces click together. During this synthesis, I will provide you with a one-page decision cheat sheet to help you connect these concepts. 

Once we have completed the walkthrough, you will move on to the graded assessment. This quiz is scenario-based by design. We are not interested in testing your knowledge of trivia; instead, these questions will place you in the seat of a practitioner who must diagnose a real-world data problem. You will be asked to determine which pattern is the right one to use and, just as importantly, how to verify that it is working correctly.
