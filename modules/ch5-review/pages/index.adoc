Goal::

Track core enablement concepts across AutoML, prompt management, and AutoRAG to validate practitioner capability in diagnosing enterprise data scenarios and selecting optimal AI patterns.

---

The interesting work happens when they combine, when a predictive model and a grounded generator sit in the same workflow and hand data back and forth. That's what this final chapter is about. 
 
First we'll walk one realistic end-to-end scenario so you can see the pieces click together, after that comes the graded assessment. It's scenario-based on purpose. Nobody's going to quiz you on trivia; the questions put you in the seat of someone diagnosing a data problem and asking "which pattern do I reach for, and how do I know it's working?"

We'll do it in two moves: a synthesis section that connects everything into a single workflow and hands you a one-page decision cheat sheet, then the quiz itself.
