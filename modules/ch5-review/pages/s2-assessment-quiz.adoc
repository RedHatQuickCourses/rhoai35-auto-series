== Interactive Assessment

Time to put it together. The 15 questions below are scenario-based: each one drops you into a situation, a CIO asking why AutoML matters, a banking team picking a task type, a RAG run with a suspicious metric combination, and asks you to make the right call. Pick an answer to see immediately whether it's right and *why*; the explanations are part of the learning, so read them even when you're sure.

You need *80% or higher* to pass. If you don't clear it the first time, revisit the relevant chapter and the cheat sheet in the previous section, then restart. There's no penalty for trying again.
