= Validating and Deploying AutoML Models

Goal::

Use AutoML evaluation artifacts (leaderboards, feature importance, ROC/PR curves) to validate business logic and promote winning models to production.

---

The tempting move is to grab whatever sits in the top row and ship it. Resist that for a few more minutes. A high score tells you a model fit the *training* data well, it doesn't yet tell you the model is measuring the *right thing*, or that it will act correctly in production.

We'll do it in three moves: first a quick look at what AutoML was actually doing while we waited, then how to read the full evaluation "report card" that comes back with every run, and finally the paved path from a winning model to a live, serving endpoint.
